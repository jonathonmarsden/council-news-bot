News

Council are proud supporters of the first performance by the Bothwell Theatre Group - an initiative of the Lions Club of Bothwell and Districts.
‘Can I Ignore You Later?’ is a comedy written and directed by a our own local playwright, Linda Boulter.

Auditions are on 16 June in the Bothwell Freemasons Lodge, with performances scheduled for Bothwell Town Hall on 24 August. Both on-stage and 
back of stage roles available. Everyone is welcome, and zero experience required.
See you there!
