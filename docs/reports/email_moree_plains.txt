To: council@mpsc.nsw.gov.au  (cc: your IT / web administrator)
Subject: Request to allow a public-interest news crawler — Moree Plains news
         is missing from the NSW council news feed

Dear Moree Plains Shire Council,

I run a free public service that republishes the news headlines of every
Australian council as links on public social media feeds, so residents see
their council's announcements without having to check the website daily.
It is the companion data service to LG News Roundup, Chris Eddy's local
government podcast, sponsored by the Victorian Local Governance
Association. You can see it at https://lgnews.jonathonmarsden.com — 129 of
the 130 NSW councils are represented.

The missing one is Moree Plains. Your website's firewall currently blocks
automated access to your media releases page, which means your community's
news — and only yours in NSW — is absent from the state feed. Every post
we publish is your headline linking to your website: the service only ever
sends readers to you.

What I am asking for is small and fully under your control: allow our
crawler through your firewall. Specifically, a rule permitting this
User-Agent:

    LGNewsRoundupBot/1.0 (+https://lgnews.jonathonmarsden.com/bot.html)

That address is a permanent reference page describing exactly what the
crawler does. In practice your logs would show one visit per day to your
media releases page — a handful of requests, less than a megabyte, from an
Australian residential connection. For detail, I've attached a technical
overview written for council IT departments, covering the architecture,
data practices, and security posture, with a verification checklist so
your team can confirm every claim independently.

Alternatives are equally welcome: if you have or would prefer an RSS feed,
an API, or a schedule of your choosing, we will fetch whatever you
nominate, however you nominate it. And if the council prefers not to
participate at all, tell me and I will remove Moree Plains from the
service permanently — the switch is entirely in your hands.

I'm a software developer and former Mayor, so I know firsthand how much
work goes into council communications and how little of it reaches
residents. This service exists to fix that, at no cost and no effort to
you.

Happy to answer any questions, or to speak with your IT team directly.

Kind regards,

Jonathon Marsden
